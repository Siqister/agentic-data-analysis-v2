# Crosswalks

Every conversion from one geography to another.

* [Block groups (2020) → City of Cleveland](bg_2020__to__cle_city.md) - Assigns every Cleveland block group, whole, to the city.
* [Census tracts (2010) → census tracts (2020), matched](tract_2010__to__tract_2020.md) - Gives a 2020 tract the value of the 2010 tract it overlaps almost completely, both ways; other 2020 tracts are blank.
